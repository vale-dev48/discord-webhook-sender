Pigeon Webhook

A tiny Windows app to send Discord messages through a webhook. Free and open source.

Download for Windows Source on GitHub
  1. Unzip and open WebhookSender.exe. No install needed.
  2. Paste your Discord webhook URL. It stays on your PC.
  3. Write your message and press Send or Ctrl+Enter.

Keep your webhook URL secret: anyone who has it can post in that channel.
